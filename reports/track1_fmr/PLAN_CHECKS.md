# Mathematical and evidence checks for the proposed plan

These checks identify assumptions that must be measured or supplied before the corresponding acceptance gates can be asserted. They do not invalidate the utility of a bounded engineering prototype.

1. **Contact and depth are not identifiable from arbitrary monocular 2-D overlap.** Different depth configurations can produce the same projection. `r_phys_prob` and `r_3d_est` therefore require a separately justified observation and calibration model; they are left unknown in the blind prototype. The source contact proposals are not independently adjudicated contact-boundary geometry.
2. **EVI is not a threshold on prior probability alone.** For a prior of 0.5 and an observation that always returns the same posterior 0.5, information gain is zero; any positive compute cost makes EVI negative. Conversely, for a prior of 0.01, a perfectly informative free observation has positive EVI. The outcome distribution and cost are necessary inputs. `foveal_lens.expected_value` requires them and checks the posterior mean equals the prior.
3. **Prediction does not always increase covariance trace.** In one dimension, a valid position/velocity covariance `[[1,-0.9],[-0.9,1]]` propagated with constant velocity for 0.5 seconds and zero process noise has trace 1.35, down from 2.0. Thus monotonic trace growth is not a general Kalman property; imposing it unconditionally requires an additional conservative rule and does not prove calibration. Shape scatter and belief covariance remain separately represented.
4. **A truncated Fourier support is not automatically a valid convex support function.** The prototype uses it for an approximate 2-D sampled screen, not a topology certificate. Only an existing certificate bound to its coefficients and decoder can authorize `CompiledGeometry`. Continuous topology and ring lineage cannot be claimed merely from a polynomial centroid or support fit.
5. **Visibility quality needs a declared range for uncertainty.** The expression `V*(1-4u)` is in `[0,1]` only under additional assumptions, including `0 <= V <= 1` and `0 <= u <= 0.25`. Depth ordering and visibility are not available merely from RGB connected components. The prototype's zero quality is an unknown-evidence marker, not measured full occlusion.
6. **Old covariance calibration does not transfer automatically.** The prior 93.8% result concerned a particular source-position representation and a small validation cohort. Flux-region centroids, changing boundaries and re-identification have different error mechanisms; their uncertainty has not been independently calibrated.
7. **A contact probability is dimensionless, while impulse has physical units.** A Track 2 control impulse cannot be obtained by substituting the former for the latter. The current collocation helper exposes position, time derivative and a generic JVP without inventing a contact control signal.
8. **Record counts do not equal storage bytes or inference cost.** The current synthetic tube comparison fits historical states retrospectively and excludes full patch/storage accounting. Its apparent record-count gain is not a measured real-video storage or latency acceptance result.

The native audit and public point-probe artifacts under this directory record the actual available evidence. Missing independent identity, motion and contact references are not filled from model-generated reviews.
