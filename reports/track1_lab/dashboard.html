The certificate uses outward-rounded Bernstein bounds, adaptive time subdivision, local injectivity and adjacent-piece tangent cones. It applies to canonical_tensor_bezier1 in normalized shape coordinates at tolerance 1e-8. The decoder has identical shared endpoints, and the reader verifies coefficient, geometry-contract and decoder-source hashes. It does not transfer the certificate to an independently evaluated SciPy curve or a raster mask. Original sampled-mask IoU tables below remain historical measurements, not newly measured canonical-decoder scores.

normalized_geometry_curves(..., require_certificate=True) rejects unresolved certificates, changed coefficients/decoder, and windows without a stable retained topology/correspondence epoch. Mathematical curve validity is separate from source completeness and identity. New fits reset their retained segment on known topology changes or ambiguous ring matching. The event ledger records annotation topology changes, not verified physical splits/merges.

Functional history and instantaneous motion now have separate responsibilities. functional_endpoint_state and functional_endpoint_velocity describe the fitted history. kinematics.position, kinematics.velocity and the joint covariance come from past observations, independently of the cubic/residual derivative. Ambiguous legacy endpoint field names are removed from the development reader.

The separate stream records 64,439 attempted 4 Hz timestamps across seven videos; 33,706 have recent-observation support. Unsupported records remain present.
